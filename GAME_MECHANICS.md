# GAME_MECHANICS

The mayor creates opportunities for autonomous elites; the city comes to depend on them and never commands them.

> **Scope.** This file is the single source of truth for the game's mechanics and the reasoning behind them. It was
> written on 2026-09-05 as a clean copy from GAME_VISION revision 5, the three vision FLOWs, three research passes and the
> owner's summary of the same day; GAME_VISION was deleted the same day and is not to be reconstructed. The owner's
> verbatim words live in `Flows/Archive/` (FLOW_BUILD_UX_DECONGESTION, FLOW_VISION_UNIFICATION, FLOW_BASIC_CITY_UI,
> FLOW_ECONOMY_POC). It says what the game does, not how code implements it (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, `ECS_CONVENTIONS.md`).
>
> **What it is not.** No balance numbers: the owner tunes them in Unity. No scenarios, no district types or names, no
> elite names, no elite characters or traits. No UI: no UI document exists; §12 holds only the model-side facts a future
> UI must honour.
>
> **Status.** Every rule is confirmed by the owner unless marked `:OPEN`. Agent proposals are marked `:agent-proposal`
> and carry a confidence number (how likely the option is the right decision, never certainty of a fact). Each rule
> block carries the date it was confirmed.

## 1 · Thesis and principles

The player is the mayor of a fantasy city. The city is the setting and the causal system; the emotional subject is a
small cast of autonomous elites whose work, buildings, households and ambitions generate observable histories. The
economy is the priority: everything else waits until the economy is functional and interesting (§16).

## 4 · Land, slots and buildings

The unit of ownership is the hex; the unit of right is the slot. The city owns every hex; the mayor develops it; an
elite holds a building in a slot by permit, never the land.

```clojure
(def hex-is-district  ;; 2026-09-02 — confirmed; :states і :slots — revision 5
  {:identity "гекс і район — один об'єкт; на гексі рівно один район"
   :owner Місто                         ;; завжди; власності й оренди еліт немає
   :mayor "розпоряджається всіма гексами: спеціалізація, дії, дозволи на слоти"
   :states #{"нерозвинений" "спеціалізований — має слоти під будівлі"}
   :specialization {:decides Мер
                    :is "шлях розвитку гексу — призначається і еволюціонує діями розвитку (def hex-development)"
                    :requires "вимоги району: місцевість, ресурс гекса, тощо (def action-precondition)"
                    :opens "N слотів під будівлі; N авторське за спеціалізацією"}
   :slot-freed "слот звільняється, коли будівля зникає — ділянка не гине разом із будівлею"   ;; 2026-09-05, урок City Tales (§15)
   :closes "одиниця власності — гекс; одиниця права — слот"})
```
